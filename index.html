⚠️ Important Safety Notice

Global Work Abroad is intended to help job seekers discover opportunities. Always verify the employer, recruitment agency, job offer, visa requirements and official application process before paying money or providing sensitive personal information. Never send money to someone simply because they promise a job or visa.
